Pater Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 16, 2026

Introduction

Pater ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Chrome browser extension ("Pater" or "Extension").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By installing or using Pater, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Watchlist Data: Product names, prices, and URLs you choose to save
  • Settings: Your preferences for notifications and display options
  • Feedback: Any feedback or communications you send to us

Information Collected Automatically

  • Product Information: When you visit supported e-commerce sites (Amazon.in, Flipkart.com, Myntra.com), we may temporarily extract product name, price, and category for price prediction purposes
  • Usage Data: Extension installation count, feature usage statistics, and error logs (no personal information)
  • Device Information: Browser type, version, and extension version for debugging purposes

🔒 Local Storage

All your watchlist data and preferences are stored locally on your device using Chrome's secure storage. We do not have access to this data on our servers.

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide AI-powered price predictions and shopping recommendations
  • Track prices and notify you of price drops
  • Sync your watchlist across your devices (if you enable cloud sync)
  • Improve our service and fix bugs
  • Respond to your feedback and support requests

What We DON'T Collect

🚫 We Never:

  • Track your general browsing history
  • Collect your login credentials, passwords, or payment information
  • Read or store data from non-product pages
  • Sell or share your personal data with third parties
  • Use your data for advertising purposes
  • Store your data on our servers without your consent

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties, except in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We may share data with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our API (hosted on Google Cloud)
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights
  • Business Transfer: In case of a merger or acquisition, your information may be transferred

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:

  • All API communications use HTTPS encryption
  • Your watchlist data is stored locally in Chrome's encrypted storage
  • API requests are authenticated with unique API keys
  • Regular security audits and updates

Data Retention

Local Data: Your watchlist and preferences are stored locally on your device. You can delete this data at any time through the extension settings or by uninstalling the extension.

Server Data: If you sync your watchlist with our API, that data is retained until you delete your account or request data deletion.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your stored data
  • Delete: Delete your data from our servers
  • Opt-out: Disable any data collection through extension settings
  • Withdraw Consent: Uninstall the extension to stop all data collection

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@pater.example.com

Children's Privacy

Pater is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete that information promptly.

International Data Transfers

If you are located outside of India, please note that your information may be transferred to and processed in India, where our servers are located. By using Pater, you consent to this transfer.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
